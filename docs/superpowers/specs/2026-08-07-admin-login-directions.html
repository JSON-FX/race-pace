The Google gate — what an unauthorized account sees
No admin access

alma@gmail.com isn't an organizer on any event yet. Ask your organization admin to invite this exact address, then sign in again.

The rule is already enforced — Google inherits it. Authorization lives in user_roles, and (admin)/layout.tsx already sends anyone without a role to /no-access. Signing in with Google authenticates; it grants nothing. An uninvited Google account reaches exactly this screen.
One thing to decide. The gate is on the EMAIL, so the invite and the Google account must use the same address. Someone invited at alma@muspo.ph who signs in with a personal alma@gmail.com is refused — correctly, but confusingly. Naming the rejected address (as above) is what turns "it's broken" into "ah, wrong account".
