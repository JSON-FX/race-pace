Five console pages — three directions

Dashboard, Check-in, Organizations, Commission and Payouts. Same information and same backend in all three; what differs is how far the console is allowed to depart from itself when the context changes. Everything is drawn with the tokens already in apps/web/app/globals.css.

A · One Console

Every new page is built from the vocabulary Registrations and Payments already use: a KPI row, then cards and tables. Nothing new to learn, nothing new to maintain, and the five pages are indistinguishable in kind from the seven that already ship. The trade is that race-day check-in gets the same desktop-office treatment as a finance table.

B · Context-Shifted

Identical to A for anything read at a desk — the dashboard and the three platform tables keep the console's exact idiom. Two contexts get a deliberate shift: check-in becomes a dark, full-bleed race-day mode built for a gloved thumb in the dark, and the platform pages wear a forest band that makes cross-org scope impossible to mistake for one org's data.

C · Bento Ops

A full data-dense redesign. The dashboard becomes an asymmetric bento of tiles sized by importance rather than a uniform KPI row, and a live activity feed runs down the side. Most striking of the three, and the furthest from what the other seven pages look like — which is both its appeal and its cost.
