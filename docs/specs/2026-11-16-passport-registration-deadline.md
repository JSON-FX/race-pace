# Race Passport requirements and approved payment deadlines

Each selected runner needs a Race Passport with identity, contact, emergency contact and shipping details before submitting for pre-screening, reserving a place or registering. These are the existing Race Passport fields. A group submission holds all selected places together. A failure on any participant rolls back the holds and queued messages.

Approval makes payment available until the event’s registration close. An event that closes by status allows approved entry payments while registration is open. For a reservation with no scheduled event close, the earliest selected category’s full-entry deadline still applies. When a scheduled event close is later, the approved runner retains enough time to convert the deposit to entry through that close.

A change to the event deadline updates live approved holds and pending checkouts together. Existing paid bookings retain their terms. Expired holds stay expired because their places may have been claimed. Provider session retries and delayed captures continue through the existing reconciliation flow.
