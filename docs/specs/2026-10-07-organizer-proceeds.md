# Organizer proceeds and active commission

Registrations displays Gross Registration and Net to organizer for the entire selected event. Gross is original captured registration charges. Net uses recorded organizer proceeds after processing, platform commission and completed refunds. Table search, category, status and pagination do not change either figure. An incomplete or failed net read displays Unavailable rather than zero.

Commission remains restricted to platform administrators. Commission Earned, its captured gross denominator and event breakdown include retained platform fees on fully refunded registrations. Current organizer proceeds continue to exclude fully refunded legacy balances. Active event commission is the captured registration commission for events currently open, almost full or coming soon. Reservation Platform Fees remain separate. No earnings are estimated from current rates, and no financial records or terms are changed.

Use existing cards, responsive registration grid and server-side parallel reads. Reuse the existing event-scoped payment aggregate; no backend migration is required.
